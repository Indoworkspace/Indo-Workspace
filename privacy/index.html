How we handle information on this website.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are

Indo Workspace is a registered MSME and pure-play Indian product company. This website, indobiz.in, is our company website. You can reach us at contact@indobiz.in.

What this website collects

This website does not collect personal information. It is a static informational site. There are no sign-up forms, no accounts, no contact forms, and we do not set any cookies of our own. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

Third-party services we use

Running the site involves a small number of external providers, and they may process technical data such as your IP address as part of delivering what the page needs:

  • GitHub Pages hosts and serves this website, and its servers may log standard request data such as IP address, browser type and the page requested.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on the site; your browser requests those font files from Google's servers.

We do not control these providers and their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

If you email us

If you write to contact@indobiz.in, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to read and reply to you, and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not sell it, and we do not add you to any marketing list.

Links to other sites

This site links to services we do not operate, including our Instagram profile and the Indoone web app. Once you follow one of those links, the privacy policy of that service applies, not this one.

The Indoone app

Indoone is a separate product with its own data practices. Its privacy policy is published with the app and applies to your use of Indoone, not to this website.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Because this website does not collect personal information, there is no stored profile to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed us and would like that correspondence removed, write to contact@indobiz.in and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@indobiz.in.
